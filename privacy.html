Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · LeaseAccom

This Privacy Policy explains how LeaseAccom collects, uses and protects your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email, phone number, account type (tenant or owner).
  • Profile and verification data — information and documents you choose to submit for identity or ownership verification.
  • Listing and activity data — properties you list or save, viewings, applications and messages.
  • Technical data — browser type, device, and basic usage logs to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To show relevant properties and enable messaging, viewings and applications
  • To review listings and verification documents (admin staff only)
  • To send important service emails (account, security, or notifications you opt into)
  • To improve the Platform and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only when needed to run the service (for example with our hosting and database provider), when required by law, or when you choose to share information with another user through the Platform (such as a viewing request or message).

4. Verification documents

Documents you upload for verification are stored securely and are visible only to authorised admin staff for review. They are not shown publicly on listings.

5. Your choices

  • You can update most account details from your dashboard.
  • You may request deletion of your account by contacting support (some records may be kept where required for legal or security reasons).
  • You can control notification preferences where those settings are available.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including access controls and encrypted connections) to protect your data. No online service can be 100% secure, so please also protect your own password.

7. Children

LeaseAccom is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Platform means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about your privacy or data, contact us through the support options on the Platform.
